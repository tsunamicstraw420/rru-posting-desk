Start here (read once)

This page is your whole week. Each card below is one post: the picture, the words, where it goes, and the day and time. Work top to bottom. When one is done, tap Done — it folds away and the pink bar at the top fills up.

Most of the week is the four groups. Those matter most, so they come first on every day. Instagram is only three posts a week now.

  1. Bookmark this page.
  2. Be logged into Facebook as yourself — the account that runs the Page and the groups.
  3. That is the whole setup.
Post in one of the groups (most of your week)
  1. Tap Copy caption on the card, then Open the group.
  2. Tap Write something… at the top of the group.
  3. Paste the words, then add the picture (Photo/Video, pick the one you saved from this page — tap the picture here, then save it).
  4. To schedule instead of posting now: look for the small calendar icon at the bottom of the box you are typing in. Tap it, set the day and time from the card, then Schedule. You can only do this in groups where you are an admin — which is all four of yours.

Every group gets slightly different words on purpose. Please use the caption on the matching card — four identical posts at once is what Facebook treats as spam.

Post on the Page (your creator dashboard)
  1. Tap Copy caption, then Open your Page dashboard.
  2. That is your Professional dashboard — the creator tools for the Page. If the button does not land you there, go to your Page and look for Professional dashboard in the menu.
  3. Find Create post (sometimes under Planner or Posts).
  4. Paste the words, add the picture.
  5. Instead of Publish, open the little arrow beside it and choose Schedule. Set the day and time from the card.

You do not need Meta Business Suite for any of this. The Page's own dashboard does it.

The first-comment trick (the Friday drop posts)

Some cards have a second, dashed box — the link to the episode. A link put inside a Facebook post gets shown to far fewer people, so it goes in the first comment instead.

  1. A comment cannot be scheduled, so this is a one-minute job on Friday morning.
  2. Just after the post goes live, open it.
  3. Tap Copy comment here, paste it as the first comment, post it.
Instagram — only three posts a week
  1. The carousel (Wednesday) — the big one. Post all six pictures in the order they appear on the card. In the Instagram app: + → select all six in order → share.
  2. The Friday feed post — one picture, paste the caption.
  3. The Story (air night) — save the tall picture, add it as a Story, then tap the sticker icon (the smiley), choose Poll, and type the same two answers that are on the picture.

Instagram cannot schedule from a computer browser, so these are done in the app on your phone, on the day.

Saving a picture to post it
  1. On a phone: press and hold the picture here, then Save — it goes to your camera roll.
  2. On a laptop: click the picture so it opens big, then right-click → Save Image As…
If something does not work
  1. Copy button does nothing? Select the words with your finger or mouse and copy them the normal way.
  2. A picture will not load? Pull down to refresh the page once.
  3. No calendar icon in a group? You are probably not signed in as an admin of that group.
  4. Anything else — message Eddie. Nothing here can break by tapping around.
