How any of this got made — the short version.
  1. Every picture is a frame taken out of an episode we animated ourselves, or one of Ruby's studio pictures. Nothing is bought, generated fresh, or taken from the TV network.
  2. A script lays our own graphics on top of that frame — background, logo, the pink lines, the lettering. Same eight layouts every week.
  3. The words come from a plain text file. Changing the words does not need new artwork — it re-draws in about fifteen seconds.
  4. You mark each one below. Nothing gets posted until you say so.

How to do it: go all the way down the page first — tap Looks good or Change this on every item, and type your note wherever you want something different. Then, once at the very end, tap the button at the bottom. You do not send after each change — one send covers the whole week. Your marks save on this device, so you can stop halfway and come back.
